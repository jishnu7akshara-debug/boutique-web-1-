Madikeri, Kodagu  /  since 1921

A planter's bungalow, kept the old way.

Eleven suites behind green shutters, a long table that serves Kodava food, and three homestays out in the coffee.

Stay type

The house was built for a coffee planter in 1921 and has had four owners since. We kept the teak floors, the lime-washed walls and every one of the shutters. What we added is quieter: deep baths, good beds, and a kitchen that cooks the way Kodava homes do.

One sitting at eight, everyone at the same teak table, unless you'd rather eat on your verandah. The menu follows whatever the kitchen garden and the Madikeri market had that morning.

Or stay out in the coffee, twenty minutes up the hill.

Three family homes on working estates, run by the families who live next door. You get the whole house, a cook if you want one, and a walk through the plantation with whoever is picking that week. Breakfast at Vellora House is always included.
